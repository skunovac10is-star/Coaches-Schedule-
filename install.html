INSTALL THE COACHES APP

Get the schedule, see who's covering, get a quick push when something changes. 60-second setup.
⏱ Takes 1 minute · 📱 iPhone & Android
1
Make sure you're in Safari
This only works in Safari — not Chrome, not Firefox. If you opened this page in another browser, copy the link and open it in Safari first.
2
Tap the Share button
At the very bottom of Safari, tap the square with an arrow pointing up ↑. It's the middle button on the bottom toolbar.
3
Scroll down → tap "Add to Home Screen"
A menu pops up. Scroll down past the icons until you see Add to Home Screen with a + icon. Tap it, then tap Add in the top-right corner.
💡You may need to scroll down inside the share sheet — the option is usually in the second row.
4
Open the new LEGACY icon from your Home Screen
Close Safari. Find the new L icon on your Home Screen — open it from there. You must open from the icon, not from Safari, otherwise push notifications won't work.
5
Enter the password
When the app opens, you'll see a password screen. Enter: Legacy2006
6
Turn on push notifications
Tap the 📣 News tab at the bottom → tap the 🔔 Push notifications sub-tab → pick your name from the dropdown → tap Save & enable notifications → tap Allow when iPhone asks.
⚠️If you don't see a permission prompt, go to iPhone Settings → Notifications → LEGACY and turn notifications on. Also make sure Focus / Do Not Disturb isn't blocking them.

4
Open the new LEGACY icon
A new icon will appear on your Home Screen. Open it from there. Password: Legacy2006
